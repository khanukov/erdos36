**Title:** A Parseval-Prefix Improvement for Erdős' Minimum-Overlap Problem

**Author:** Dmitry Khanukov

**Abstract:** We give a preliminary computer-assisted argument that the
asymptotic constant in Erdős' minimum-overlap problem satisfies
`c_E > 0.3805603`. Parseval energy constraints were already used by White.
The contribution claimed here is their finite profile-space prefix projection,
its use to replace the two binding central bins of a public 172-bin
certificate, and the corresponding directed-rounding MPFR verification. The
remaining 170 bins use SHA-256-pinned reports from Price's Arb computation;
that Arb computation is not rerun by this package. The result is unrefereed,
has not been independently reproduced for the new central pair, and is not
Lean-verified.

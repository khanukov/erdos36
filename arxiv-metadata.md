**Title:** A Parseval-Prefix Improvement for Erdős' Minimum-Overlap Problem

**Author:** Dmitry Khanukov

**Abstract:** We give a preliminary computer-assisted argument that the
asymptotic constant in Erdős' minimum-overlap problem satisfies
`c_E > 0.3805603`. The new ingredient is a finite Parseval-prefix inequality
used to replace the two binding central bins of a public 172-bin certificate.
A directed-rounding MPFR program checks the new central positive-part
integral. The remaining 170 bins use SHA-256-pinned reports from Price's Arb
computation; that Arb computation is not rerun by this package. The result is
unrefereed, has not been independently reproduced for the new central pair,
and is not Lean-verified.

**Comments:** Preliminary and unrefereed; 5 pages. Source, certificate,
verifier, and exact trust boundary: https://github.com/khanukov/erdos36
